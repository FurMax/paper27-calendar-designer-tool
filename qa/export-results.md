## Session 08 JPG choice and photo-edge correction

The Product Owner supplied January and May print JPGs with a visible pale border. Pixel inspection found January white through approximately row 53, about 19 px inside the nominal 35 px top bleed/trim boundary; both files showed a brightened right edge. This is not explained solely by the required 3 mm bleed. The old renderer also stretched one antialiased trim-edge pixel across the 36 px right bleed, which could amplify a fringe. Missing source Blobs/crop states mean the exact share of source-photo margin and renderer sampling in those historical files cannot be separated. Photo pixel count by itself is not the cause: a covered low-resolution photo is resampled and may be soft, but should still fill the region.

The previous reflected-strip correction was superseded after the Product Owner supplied February/March JPGs showing repeated content. The renderer now uses genuine source-photo pixels across print bleed and shows the tighter crop in print Preview. `session08-photo-edge.mjs` passed in isolated Chrome and Edge: solid-source digital/print PNG/JPG edge samples, including all print photo corners and 35/36 px bleed edges, matched the source color; a steep red gradient continued across the right trim edge into genuine source-photo bleed rather than becoming a flat or reflected band; a 50 px white source margin was warned in digital crop at zoom 1 and cleared at zoom 1.3, while the derived print crop already excluded it. `session08-photo-edge-ui.mjs` passed in Chrome: a 120 px synthetic white source margin caused the full-set warning to name all twelve affected months before rendering, linked to the Month Editor, dropped January after its zoom change and allowed intentional pale edges to continue to ZIP readiness; the month list scrolled without horizontal overflow in 320 CSS px emulation. This is a bounded fixture result; real supplied photos cannot be regenerated without original source/crop data, and iPhone/iPad Safari output after this correction is not yet retested.

JPG integration in isolated Windows Chrome and Edge passed: PNG remained default; JPG selection generated twelve ordered print JPGs in one ZIP with valid SOF 1252×1843, 300 dpi JFIF density and twelve unique hashes; single digital JPG was 1200×1800. The format selector fit a 320 CSS px emulation without horizontal overflow. `jpeg-export.test.ts` covered marker/density mutation and ZIP validation. The old PNG integration suite still passed. The new JPG sample matches the Product Owner's stated format, 300 dpi, RGB allowance and nominal bleed geometry.
