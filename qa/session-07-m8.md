## Product Owner implementation review follow-up — 2026-09-23

The Product Owner reported no problems with (1) selecting/assigning twelve photos, (2) crop and style editing, (3) refresh/restore, (4) one-month PNG, and (5) full-set ZIP download on iPhone Safari. Device model, iOS/Safari version, source-file sizes and actual PNG files were not supplied. This is a **reported scoped workflow PASS**, not the formal Session 08 browser-matrix result.

The Product Owner explicitly noted that the phone received a ZIP, with no continuous twelve-image download option. In a later follow-up, the Product Owner confirmed that iPhone ZIP extraction worked. This is a **reported scoped iPhone extraction PASS**; the exact device/OS version and inspection of each of the twelve PNGs were not recorded. Apple's Files documentation also describes iPhone ZIP extraction: https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/102532 and https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/guide/iphone/iphab82e0798/ios . The approved plan deliberately avoids twelve automatic browser downloads; a usable mobile multi-file primary handoff still needs trusted-HTTPS device evidence.

Google's Files by Google documentation says an Android user can open a `.zip`, tap **Extract**, and find the extracted files beside the original archive: https://support.google.com/files/answer/9048509?hl=en . The app is available on Android 8.0 and later: https://support.google.com/files/answer/9848742?hl=en . This establishes a documented Android extraction path, not a PASS for this product's download, file-manager, twelve-PNG inspection, or Chrome/Android matrix. Those remain **NOT TESTED** on a real Android device.

The Product Owner also found the native eyedropper imprecise, suggested improving the palette and asked for font color adjustment. Calendar text color already has per-month Auto/Custom plus a color picker and HEX input. The mobile entry and precision hints were clarified without changing the approved control model; `tests/browser/m8-color-discovery.mjs` verified the route at 320px without horizontal overflow. Palette redesign and any different color scope are documented as an unapproved [UI/UX change request](../design/ui-ux-change-request-session-07.md).

The Product Owner subsequently reported that the three text-size choices are too similar and that the native background eyedropper does not precisely select colors from the photo. A 390px browser inspection measured proof title sizes of **20.24 / 23 / 25.76 px** for Small / Standard / Large, reflecting the current `0.88 / 1 / 1.12` multipliers. Weekdays were **10.56 / 12 / 13.44 px** and dates **11.44 / 13 / 14.56 px**. This confirms a modest distinction; it does not approve a new scale. The proposed changes and checks are in the [UI/UX change request](../design/ui-ux-change-request-session-07.md).
The requested 3 mm print bleed and 1270 × 1870 px candidate are documented in the [print-bleed change request](../design/ui-ux-change-request-print-bleed-session-07.md). At the time of that proposal, V1 PNGs remained 1200 × 1800 px and were not claimed print-ready; the approved revision is documented below.
